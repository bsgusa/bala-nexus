## Vision

**bala-nexus is the connective tissue.**

This repository is the central map of Shiv Balamonis's builder surface — every repo, every venture, every LLC, every open thread. If it exists in the Balamonis world, it is indexed here with its owner, its role, its status, and how to connect to it.

We do not claim certifications we do not hold. We do not fabricate customers, funding, or results. What you see is what is real: the repos that exist, the work in progress, and the doors open for collaboration.

## Public engagement

- **Issues** = the front door. Propose a collab, report a gap, suggest a connection.
- **Discussions** = open conversation about the direction of the new world.
- **Pull requests** = welcome. See `bala-weave` for contribution templates and good-first-issues.
- Every repo in this map carries its own `AGENTS.md` and `backlog/priorities.md` so any contributor can start in 60 seconds.

## Working rules (inherit from Balamonis Group)

- **Work on a branch, never `main`.** Small commits with clear messages.
- **Truthful claims only.** No fabricated certifications, customers, funding, or models.
- **Compliance requires evidence.** An item is `verified` only with an evidence pointer.
- **Before ending a session:** update `ROADMAP.md`, `backlog/priorities.md`, append to `reports/LOG.md`.

## Getting started

1. Pick a repo in the map above that interests you.
2. Read its `AGENTS.md` first.
3. Open an issue in `bala-voice` to say you're joining, or fork `bala-weave` to contribute.
4. Build in public. Ship in public. Connect in public.
